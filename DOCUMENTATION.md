# Nexora Free Lite Documentation

Nexora is a static multi-page IT and digital transformation company template.

## Customize

1. Replace the fictional company, contact, client, review, and news content.
2. Replace the logo, favicon, Open Graph image, and demonstration photography.
3. Edit the navy and orange theme values in `assets/css/style.css`.
4. Connect the demonstration form in `assets/js/main.js` to a secure endpoint.
5. Replace `example.com` links and update canonical URLs for your domain.
6. Change `noindex, nofollow`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` before production indexing.

The pages use Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, and Google Fonts from public CDNs. No build step or backend is included.
